## About me

I'm Harper Moreira and I'm a brazilian 21 years old generalist artist, Computer Science student and game developer.

I am the co-founder of the SALTO Gamedev university extension group at the Federal Institute of São Paulo, where I am also studying Computer Science and am a former undergraduate research scholarship recipient in gamification.

Currently, I direct a game requested by the Special Department of Federal Revenue of Brazil within the group I co-founded.

I have been using Linux for 5 years and I like it a lot though I still use Windows for music composition. I was a long-time Arch and Gentoo Linux user and currently use Fedora.
